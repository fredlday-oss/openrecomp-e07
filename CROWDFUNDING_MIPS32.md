# OpenRecomp MIPS32 crowdfunding campaign

The OpenRecomp MIPS32 hardening campaign is live on Crowdfunder:

**https://www.crowdfunder.co.uk/p/openrecomp-mips32**

## What the campaign funds

The campaign funds a bounded MIPS32 hardening and validation programme covering:

- ingestion and extraction hardening;
- MIPS32 semantics and translation;
- bounded indirect-control-flow work;
- native and WebAssembly parity/repeatability;
- rights-safe fixtures and negative tests; and
- public documentation and evidence.

The public target is **£10,500**, structured to fund a **£10,000 net engineering programme** plus transaction-fee headroom.

## Evidence boundary

OpenRecomp already has a **PROVEN RV32I synthetic baseline** within its documented bounded scope.

MIPS32 is not starting from zero: bounded MIPS32 fixtures and validation paths already exist and pass their documented tests. Those results do **not** establish arbitrary MIPS32 compatibility, so wider/general MIPS32 support remains **CANDIDATE**.

**Funding does not make MIPS32 PROVEN. Evidence does.**

The campaign funds the engineering and validation needed to attempt to move the documented MIPS32 scope toward PROVEN. If the required validation does not succeed, the failures and remaining limitations will be documented instead.

Existing work will not be rebilled as new campaign work.

## Rights and reproducibility

Public campaign evidence will use synthetic, open, homebrew, public-domain or otherwise authorised material as appropriate. The campaign does not fund distribution of copyrighted games, proprietary firmware, console keys or proprietary SDK material.

The aim is to publish reproducible tests, bounded claims and public evidence that can be independently inspected and challenged.
