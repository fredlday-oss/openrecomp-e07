# Code of conduct

OpenRecomp contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful, specific and evidence-driven.

Disagreement about architecture, reverse engineering, validation or proof claims is welcome when it focuses on reproducible facts and the work itself.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
