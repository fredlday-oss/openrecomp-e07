# OpenRecomp Sponsors

OpenRecomp is an independent open-source research and engineering project focused on architecture-neutral static recompilation, deterministic validation and reproducible evidence.

Sponsorship helps fund the engineering work required to keep the project moving, including:

- development hardware and high-memory systems
- local and cloud compute
- cross-platform testing and verification
- compiler, architecture and runtime development
- CI and reproducibility infrastructure
- public documentation and evidence
- rights-safe test programs and fixtures

## Sponsorship principles

Sponsorship supports the work. It does **not** determine technical outcomes, compatibility claims, evidence classifications, project governance or PASS/FAIL results.

OpenRecomp remains evidence-driven: technical claims are accepted only when backed by executable tests, runtime evidence, reproducible results or explicit review.

## Sponsor acknowledgement

OpenRecomp is supported by individuals and organisations helping fund open-source static recompilation research and engineering.

Sponsors who choose to be publicly recognised may be acknowledged here and in project communications.

Sponsorship does not confer influence over evidence classifications, technical conclusions or project governance.
