# OpenRecomp MIPS32 crowdfunding campaign

The OpenRecomp MIPS32 hardening campaign is live on Crowdfunder:

https://www.crowdfunder.co.uk/p/openrecomp-mips32

The campaign funds work on the existing **MIPS32 CANDIDATE** path: ingestion/extraction hardening, semantics and translation, bounded indirect control flow, native/WebAssembly parity and repeatability, rights-safe validation fixtures, and public documentation/evidence.

The engineering programme is deliberately evidence-led. **Funding does not make MIPS32 PROVEN. Evidence does.** Existing bounded MIPS32 results remain evidence for their documented fixtures and scopes only; they are not a claim of arbitrary MIPS32 compatibility.

Existing work is not being rebilled as new campaign work. Public validation remains limited to synthetic, open, homebrew, public-domain or otherwise authorised material as appropriate. The campaign does not fund distribution of copyrighted games, proprietary firmware, console keys or proprietary SDK material.

The public campaign target is £10,500. The underlying engineering programme is £10,000 net, with the public target accounting for platform transaction fees.

Preserve. Understand. Recompile. Keep software running.
